💀 Tournament Structure

Bananium tournaments are where the chaos of the Colosseum comes to life. Every Sunday, the champions of Season 1 face off in a fully automated battle royale, streamed live for the community to witness. Each clash isn't just about luck of the draw, it's about preparation, training, and the collective effort of every holder rallying behind their fighter.

📅 Seasons & Weekly Flow

Each Season lasts 3 months, with weekly tournaments serving as the heartbeat of the competition, leading up to a grand finale where a victor is crowned. Players don't just watch, they rally behind their chosen fighter as part of a team of NFT holders who share the same character.

Here's how it works:

🍌 147 Unique Fighters: While the collection includes thousands of NFTs, only 147 unique fighters step into the arena each week, each fighter represents a "squad" of holders.

🏟️ Your Squad, Your Fighter: Think of it like supporting your football club. Even though many people own the same character, only one enters the battle each week, and it fights on behalf of every holder in that squad. If they win, the rewards will be divided amongst the holders.

🎮 Weekly Cadence: Battles take place every Sunday. The following Monday, the Training Ground opens, where players compete in skill-based mini-games to influence how their fighter will perform in the next week's tournament.

By combining squad loyalty, weekly spectacle, and seasonal arcs, Bananium tournaments create an evolving competition where every holder has a stake in the outcome, and the bragging rights that come with victory.

🏆 Reward Pool

🏟️ Fueling the Colosseum

At the heart of every clash in the Bananium Colosseum lies the Reward Pool — the treasury that powers competition, victory, and bragging rights. From the very beginning, 15% of mint funds are allocated directly into this pool, ensuring the ecosystem has a dedicated, transparent source of rewards. But unlike traditional "jackpots," the Reward Pool is designed to grow, evolve, and continuously feed back into the community.

🔥 Weekly Tournaments

Each Sunday, the Colosseum ignites. Fighters, representing their NFT holders, battle for glory. Winners of the weekly tournament claim a share of the Reward Pool, distributed across both:

👑 Champion Rewards: Direct allocations to the winning fighters' communities in the Colosseum

🎯 Training Rewards: Bonuses tied to performance in the Training Grounds, rewarding skill-based play and collective effort

🏅 Seasonal Finals

Every three months, a Season Champion is crowned. Seasonal rewards are larger, drawn from a dedicated reserve within the Reward Pool. This ensures that both short-term bursts of competition and long-term strategies matter.

🔎 Transparent & Sustainable

Allocations from the Reward Pool are publicly trackable and distributed automatically. This ensures fairness, while keeping the focus on gameplay rather than speculation.


By anchoring the ecosystem in a dedicated reward structure, every battle matters, every season counts, and every NFT holder has a stake in the outcome.

📖 Bananium Glossary

(Because even degens need to sound smart sometimes)

  • Airdrop ✈️

    A distribution method where tokens or NFTs are sent directly to users' wallets without requiring a purchase or exchange.

  • Blockchain ⛓️

    A decentralized, digital ledger technology that records transactions across a network of computers, ensuring transparency and immutability.

  • Cryptocurrency 💰

    A digital or virtual currency secured by cryptography, operating independently of central banks (e.g., Bitcoin, Solana).

  • DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization) 🏛️

    A community-governed entity where decisions are made through token-based voting systems.

  • Fiat Currency 💵

    Government-issued legal tender (e.g., USD, EUR) not backed by physical commodities.

  • Gas Fees ⛽

    Transaction costs required to execute operations on a blockchain network.

  • Governance 🗳️

    The process by which stakeholders vote on changes or updates to a blockchain protocol or platform.

  • Holder 🤲

    An individual or entity that owns tokens or NFTs in their blockchain wallet.

  • Humiliatum 💀

    A rare, high-impact move within Bananium's battle system designed to eliminate opponents decisively.

  • Leaderboard 🏆

    A ranked list of participants based on performance metrics (e.g., battle wins, prediction points, Training Ground scores).

  • Meme Immortality 😂

    A state where a losing AI agent's defeat becomes a widely recognized meme within the community.

  • Mint 🪙

    The process of creating or purchasing a new NFT or token on a blockchain.

  • NFT (Non-Fungible Token) 🎨

    A unique, indivisible digital asset stored on a blockchain, representing ownership of items like art, collectibles, or in-game characters.

  • Oracle of the Colosseum 🔮

    The prediction system where players forecast battle outcomes using points. Compete for leaderboards, badges, and bragging rights — Oracle points never carry monetary value and cannot be exchanged for anything with real monetary value.

  • Protocol 📡

    A set of rules governing how data is transmitted and validated on a blockchain network.

  • Season 1 ⚔️

    The inaugural 3-month competitive phase of Bananium. Each week, live-streamed battles determine winners, culminating in seasonal champions. Supply is capped at 4,444 fighters.

  • Shadow Realm 🌑

    A term describing the elimination of a participant from the competition, rendering their assets inactive.

  • Smart Contract 🤖

    Self-executing code on a blockchain that automates actions (e.g., distributing rewards) when predefined conditions are met.

  • SOL ☀️

    The native cryptocurrency of the Solana blockchain, used for transactions and fees within Bananium.

  • Staking 🔒

    Locking tokens in a blockchain network to support operations (e.g., validation) in exchange for rewards.

  • Token 🎟️

    A digital asset issued on a blockchain, often representing utility, ownership, or access rights.

  • Training Ground 🏋️

    A competitive hub for skill-based mini-games. Performance here influences fighter stats and determines eligibility for monetary rewards in compliance with skill-based gaming rules.

  • Transaction 📝

    A recorded action on a blockchain, such as transferring tokens or minting an NFT.

  • Ultimatium ⚡

    A powerful, game-ending ability within Bananium's battle system.

  • Volatility 📉📈

    The degree of price fluctuation in cryptocurrencies or tokens over time.

  • Wallet 🔑

    A digital tool for storing, sending, and receiving cryptocurrencies and NFTs.

  • $POT 🍌

    Bananium's native cryptocurrency token, created under the project's IP. It exists as a standard crypto asset with no in-game utility or wagering function.
